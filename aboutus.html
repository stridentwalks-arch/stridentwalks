Who are we?

Strident Walks is a virtual walking experience website dedicated to making walking accessible, enjoyable, and immersive for everyone through virtual walking experiences.


We offer a wide range of virtual walking videos that allow users to explore different locations and environments from the comfort of their own homes. Our videos are designed for everyone including beginners, seniors, and individuals with disabilities.


Our videos will help you discover the world at a pace that suits your lifestyle. Whether you're looking to stay active, explore new places, or simply enjoy the beauty of nature, Strident Walks has something for everyone.
